## Detailed transport notes

### Sydney

- Sydney Airport to CBD: 25–40 minutes by taxi/rideshare, depending on traffic.
- CBD to Circular Quay / Opera House: usually 10–20 minutes on foot or by local transport.
- Circular Quay to Manly: 20–30 minutes by ferry.
- Circular Quay to Taronga Zoo: about 12 minutes by ferry, then a short walk or cable car up to the zoo entrance.
- Sydney to Blue Mountains: a full-day trip; allow approximately 8–10 hours in total including travel and attractions.

### Sydney to Melbourne — Monday, 12 April

- Allow approximately 4–5 hours door to door:
    - Hotel to Sydney Airport: 25–40 minutes
    - Airport check-in/security: about 1½ hours
    - Flight: about 1 hour 35 minutes
    - Melbourne Airport to hotel: 30–45 minutes
- Pick up your rental car at Melbourne Airport after landing. This is convenient, but make sure your Melbourne hotel includes reasonably priced parking.

### Melbourne regional drives

- Melbourne to Puffing Billy: about 1–1¼ hours each way.
- Melbourne to Healesville Sanctuary: about 1–1½ hours each way.
- Melbourne to Phillip Island: usually 2–2½ hours, depending on your hotel location and traffic.
- Cowes to Penguin Parade: around 10–15 minutes.
- Cowes to Nobbies: around 15–20 minutes.
- Phillip Island to Melbourne Airport: allow 2½–3 hours on departure day.

## Friday, 16 April: Melbourne to Phillip Island

This is the best day to move hotels because you will sleep on Phillip Island after the Penguin Parade.

Suggested timing:

- 9:00–10:00 AM: Check out of Melbourne hotel.
- 10:00 AM–12:00 PM: Drive toward Phillip Island.
- Optional stop: Moonlit Sanctuary, where your child can see and interact with Australian wildlife including kangaroos.
- Afternoon: Check in around Cowes; rest, eat, and prepare warm clothes.
- Late afternoon: Nobbies boardwalk.
- Evening: Penguin Parade.
- Night: Return to Cowes hotel in about 10–15 minutes—no long, tiring drive back to Melbourne.

## Saturday, 17 April: Phillip Island to Melbourne Airport

- Morning: Cowes Beach, Koala Conservation Reserve, or a relaxed café/brunch.
- Early afternoon: lunch and final packing.
- Leave Cowes around 5:00 PM.
- Arrive at Melbourne Airport around 7:30–8:00 PM, allowing time for fuel, rental-car return, baggage check-in, and security.
- TG462 departure: 11:30 PM.
